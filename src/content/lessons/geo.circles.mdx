## Standard equation

A circle centered at <M t="(h,k)" /> with radius <M t="r" /> has equation <M t="(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2" />. The signs inside the parentheses are opposite the center coordinates. The right side is the radius squared.

## Other circle measures

Circumference is <M t="C=2\pi r" /> and area is <M t="A=\pi r^2" />. Arc length and sector area are the matching fraction of the full circumference and area; use the central angle over <M t="360^\circ" /> (or radians over <M t="2\pi" />).

## Common traps

- Reading the center's signs exactly as they appear inside the parentheses.
- Reporting <M t="r^2" /> as the radius instead of taking the square root.
- Using diameter where a formula calls for radius.

## Desmos steps

1. Enter `(x-2)^2+(y+3)^2=25`; Desmos draws the circle.
2. Compare the equation with standard form: the center is `(2,-3)` and the radius is `sqrt(25)=5`.
3. You can check circumference with `2*pi*r` or area with `pi*r^2`; keep the answer in terms of <M t="\pi" /> unless a decimal is requested.
