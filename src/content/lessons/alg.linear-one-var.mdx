## A reliable order

The goal is to get <M t="x" /> by itself. First undo addition or subtraction, then undo multiplication or division. Whatever you do to one side, do to the other. If parentheses are present, distribute before combining like terms.

## Check the value

Substitute your value for the variable in the **original** equation. Both sides should give the same number. This catches sign and arithmetic slips.

## Common traps

- Changing a sign when moving a term without doing the same operation on both sides.
- Dividing by only part of a coefficient expression.
- Checking in a simplified equation instead of the original one.

## Desmos check

1. Enter the left side as `y = 3x + 4`.
2. Enter the right side as `y = 19`.
3. Find their intersection. Its <M t="x" />-coordinate is the equation's solution. Substitute it back in to confirm.

Desmos is a quick check; the written steps show why the solution works.
