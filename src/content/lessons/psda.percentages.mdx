## Turn a percent into a multiplier

Convert a percent to a decimal by dividing by 100. To find <M t="p\%" /> of an amount, multiply by <M t="p/100" />. For an increase, use the multiplier <M t="1+p/100" />; for a decrease, use <M t="1-p/100" />.

## Find the starting amount

If a final amount is known, divide by the change multiplier to recover the original. A 20% increase followed by a 20% decrease does not return to the start, because the second percent is taken from the new amount.

## Common traps

- Using the percent number as a whole number instead of dividing by 100.
- Reporting only the increase instead of the new total.
- Adding successive percent changes when each applies to a different base.

## Desmos check

1. Enter `new=original*(1+rate/100)` for an increase, or change `+` to `-` for a decrease.
2. Substitute the original amount and rate to check your arithmetic.
3. For an unknown original, solve `final=x*(1+rate/100)` and inspect the intersection with `y=final`.
