## Angle relationships

Angles on a straight line add to <M t="180^\circ" />. Vertical angles are equal. When parallel lines are crossed by a transversal, corresponding and alternate interior angles are equal, while same-side interior angles add to <M t="180^\circ" />.

## Triangle facts

The interior angles of every triangle add to <M t="180^\circ" />. An exterior angle equals the sum of the two remote interior angles. Similar triangles have equal matching angles and proportional matching side lengths.

## Common traps

- Using a parallel-line angle rule when parallel lines are not given.
- Matching the wrong corresponding sides in similar triangles.
- Forgetting that the third angle must still be positive.

## Desmos check

1. Enter an equation such as `x+64+53=180` to check the missing angle arithmetic.
2. If a diagram gives a relationship such as vertical angles, write that relationship before entering the equation.
3. Desmos can solve for the variable, but the diagram determines which angles are equal or supplementary.
