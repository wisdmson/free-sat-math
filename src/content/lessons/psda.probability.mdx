## Basic probability

When outcomes are equally likely, <M t="P(A)=\text{favorable outcomes}/\text{all outcomes}" />. A probability is between 0 and 1. A fraction, decimal, or percent can express the same probability.

## Conditional probability

The phrase “given that” changes the group you are counting from. In <M t="P(A\mid B)" />, count outcomes where both A and B happen, then divide by the outcomes where B happens. Use the B row or column total as the denominator.

## Common traps

- Using the whole sample as the denominator after the question gives a condition.
- Adding probabilities when events overlap without subtracting the overlap.
- Assuming outcomes are equally likely when the problem does not say so.
