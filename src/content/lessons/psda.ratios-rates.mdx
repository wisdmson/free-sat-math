## Find a unit rate

A ratio compares quantities. A rate compares quantities with different units. To find a unit rate, divide both quantities by the same amount until one is 1, and keep the resulting units attached to the answer.

## Scale proportionally

In a proportional relationship, <M t="y=kx" /> and the ratio <M t="y/x" /> stays constant. Use conversion factors so units cancel: for example, multiply miles by <M t="5280\text{ feet}/1\text{ mile}" /> when converting to feet.

## Common traps

- Flipping a rate and reporting minutes per page instead of pages per minute.
- Adding quantities when the relationship calls for scaling.
- Converting a squared or cubed unit without squaring or cubing the conversion factor.

## Desmos steps

1. Enter `y=12x` when the unit rate is 12 units per minute.
2. Enter the desired input, such as `x=5`; the intersection shows the predicted output.
3. Keep units on your written work. Desmos calculates numbers but does not know whether you entered minutes, hours, or seconds.
