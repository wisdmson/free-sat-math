## Describe the relationship

A scatterplot shows paired measurements. Describe its direction (positive, negative, or none), form (often roughly linear), and strength. A line of best fit summarizes a trend; its slope describes predicted change in <M t="y" /> for a one-unit increase in <M t="x" />.

## Predict within the data

Substitute an x-value into the model to make a prediction. Predictions inside the observed x-range are interpolation. Predictions far outside that range are extrapolation and may be unreliable. Association by itself does not establish that one variable caused the other.

## Common traps

- Reversing which variable the slope describes.
- Treating a prediction as an exact observed value.
- Extending a line far beyond the data without checking whether the pattern still makes sense.

## Desmos steps

1. Put x-values in table column `x_1` and matching y-values in `y_1`.
2. Enter `y_1~mx_1+b` on a new line to fit a linear model; Desmos estimates `m` and `b`.
3. Use the model to predict within the data range, and label any extrapolation as uncertain.
