## Estimate with a margin

A statistic describes a sample. A confidence interval uses that estimate plus and minus a margin of error to give a range of plausible population values. A larger sample often reduces sampling variability, but the margin also depends on the data and confidence level.

## Interpret the range

For an estimate of 62% with a margin of 4 percentage points, the interval is 58% to 66%. The margin is in **percentage points**. It does not mean 4% of 62. A confidence interval is not a guarantee that every individual response falls in the range.

## Common traps

- Confusing percentage points with a percent change.
- Describing a sample statistic as if it were the exact population value.
- Claiming a confidence interval covers individual observations.

## Desmos check

2. Use the same units for the estimate and margin; if one is a percent, both should be percentages.
