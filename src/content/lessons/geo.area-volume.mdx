## Match the measure to the shape

Area measures a flat region in square units. Volume measures space inside a solid in cubic units. For a rectangular prism, <M t="V=lwh" />. For a cylinder, <M t="V=\pi r^2h" />. Write the formula before substituting values.

## Watch scale factors

If every length is multiplied by <M t="k" />, an area is multiplied by <M t="k^2" /> and a volume by <M t="k^3" />. Keep the units attached: a volume of 48 means 48 cubic units, not square units.

## Common traps

- Using a perimeter formula when the question asks for area.
- Forgetting to square the radius in circle area or cylinder volume.
- Reporting linear, square, and cubic units interchangeably.

## Desmos check

1. Enter `V=l*w*h` and substitute the three dimensions to check the product.
2. For a circle-based formula, enter `pi*r^2` or `pi*r^2*h` and keep `pi` exact until a decimal is requested.
3. Use the formula sheet to choose the geometric relationship; Desmos does not decide which dimensions belong in it.
